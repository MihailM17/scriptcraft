# ScriptCraft v0.8.15

Released: 2026-11-24

> macOS DMG in this release is **ad-hoc signed** (no Developer ID/notarisation).
> Windows/Linux artifacts are unsigned. The in-app installer verifies the
> download and replaces the app, but it cannot bypass Gatekeeper/SmartScreen
> until those builds are signed. This is not a silent updater and not a
> notarised distribution.

## Highlights

v0.8.15 brings a comprehensive native UI polish across the entire studio, adds inline **Bold / Italic / Underline** formatting across the editor, PDF export, and all screenplay interchange formats, introduces opt-in **US Letter** page support (keeping A4 as the default), and upgrades the Revisions Hub, Compare Drafts workspace, and Find & Replace.

### 1. Native Studio UI Polish

- **Start, Script, Cards, Statistics & Export:** Refined visual hierarchy, contextual top toolbar, quiet borderless Elements rail with keycaps, responsive Corkboard index cards, single-accent Statistics KPI strip and editorial tables, and a cleanly structured Export Scenario dialog.
- **Menu Popup, Research, Compare & Dialogs:** Compact `264px` Menu popup with uppercase section headers (`PROJECT`, `DRAFT & INTERCHANGE`, `APPLICATION`), hairlines, and glyph-safe `Shift+⌘` shortcuts; slimmer `272px` Research tree with `720px`-constrained setting cards; segmented `Unified` / `Side by side` Compare header with non-overlapping change badges; redesigned `About ScriptCraft`, `Help`, and `Clear Revision History` dialogs.

### 2. Inline Bold, Italic & Underline

- **Live Editor & Shortcuts:** Press `⌘B` / `⌘I` / `⌘U` (`Ctrl+B/I/U`) or click the `B` / `I` / `U` pills in the Script toolbar to toggle inline formatting on any selection (or the word under the caret). Spans shift cleanly across edits, splits, merges, and undo/redo while staying locked to the exact monospace cell grid.
- **PDF & Interchange Round-Trip:** Inline styles render in exported PDFs using bundled italic faces (including Cyrillic fallback via Liberation Mono Italic), optical bold-italic weight, and underline strokes, and persist cleanly across `.kitsp` (native KIT `<formatting>` XML), `.fdx` (`<Text Style="…">`), `.fadein` (OSF `<text style="…">`), and `.fountain` (`**bold**`, `*italic*`, `_underline_`).

### 3. Opt-In US Letter Page Support (A4 Stays Default)

- **A4 (`58` lines/page) remains the default** for KIT Scenarist compatibility.
- Switch to Hollywood-standard **US Letter (`8.5 × 11 in`, `55` lines/page)** in **Settings → Appearance → Page size**, or override per export in **Export Scenario → Template**. Both live editor geometry/pagination and PDF export follow the selected `PageSpec` 1:1.

### 4. Revisions Hub, Wrapped Compare Diffs, Find & Replace & macOS File Associations

- **Revisions Hub & Summary:** Redesigned `Research ▸ Versions` with an inline revision timeline; clickable Revision Summary rows jump directly to the changed scene in the editor.
- **Wrapped Compare Diffs:** Long paragraphs wrap cleanly in both Unified and Side-by-Side Compare modes.
- **Find & Replace:** Added Match Case (`Aa`), Whole Word (`W`), and empty-string replacement to delete matches cleanly.
- **macOS File Associations:** Registered `.kitsp`, `.fdx`, `.fadein`, and `.fountain` in `Info.plist` so double-clicking scripts in Finder opens them directly in ScriptCraft.
