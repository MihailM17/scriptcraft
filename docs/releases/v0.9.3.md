# Kino Scenarist v0.9.3

## Highlights

- **Autocomplete Cursor Placement:** Clicking or pressing `Enter`/`Tab` on an autocomplete suggestion (such as character names or scene headings) now places the cursor cleanly at the end of the completed text and keeps editor focus so pressing `Enter` moves directly into the next element without splitting the name.
- **Delete Key on Floor-Plan Props & Actors:** Clicking a prop or actor on the Shots 2D floor plan and pressing `Delete` (or `Backspace`) immediately removes it without interfering when typing inside text fields.
- **Shots Tab Undo & Redo (`Ctrl/Cmd+Z` & `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`):** Full undo/redo history in the Shots workspace covering shots, script assignments, beat notes, and all floor-plan edits (with gesture coalescing for dragging and typing), plus a fix ensuring `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z` Redo works reliably across the whole app.
