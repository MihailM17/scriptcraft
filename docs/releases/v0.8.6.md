# ScriptCraft v0.8.6

Released: 2026-11-16 (unreleased at the time of writing)

> macOS DMG remains **ad-hoc signed** (no Developer ID/notarisation).
> Windows/Linux artifacts are unsigned. Do not claim signed/notarised
> distribution. Auto-update is deferred.

## Highlights

v0.8.6 is the writing surface the way a writer expects it: every space you
type stays where you typed it, Latin and Cyrillic share one type design, the
paper can print plain black on white while the rest of the app keeps its
theme, Tab offers the story's characters instead of making you retype names,
and Research lists what the script already contains.

### Typing

- **Every space keeps its own cell.** A run of spaces used to render as a
  single cell, so a second or third space was invisible and the caret could
  not sit between them. Pages now show the source text exactly as typed:
  consecutive spaces, leading spaces and trailing spaces all survive, in the
  editor and in the exported PDF. Only the run that causes a line wrap is
  swallowed by the break, as before. Single-space paragraphs paginate
  byte-identically to previous releases.

### Page typography

- **IBM Plex Mono is the default page face.** Courier Prime has no Cyrillic
  glyphs, so each Cyrillic character was being drawn by a different font with
  a different x-height and baseline — the reason Latin looked smaller and
  higher on the same line. IBM Plex Mono draws both scripts in one design at
  the same metrics. Courier Prime remains available as the "classic, Latin
  only" face.
- **Choose the page face.** Settings ▸ Scenario Editor ▸ Page offers the two
  bundled faces with a live sample. The editor and the exported PDF follow the
  choice. The page grid (12 pt / 0.6 em) does not change, so no page break
  moves between faces.
- **Monochrome paper.** Theme ▸ Monochrome paper prints a white page with
  black ink while panels, menus and toolbars keep the selected palette. Caret,
  selection, current-line band and page numbers switch to ink and grey;
  revision marks stay coloured.

### Writing flow

- **Tab picks a character.** Press Tab in an empty character cue and the
  story's characters open in a dropdown: arrows move the highlight, Enter or
  Tab commits, Escape closes and leaves the cue empty. Parenthetical
  extensions are folded, so `ИВАН` and `ИВАН (V.O.)` are one choice. Research
  characters that the script has not used yet are offered after the script's
  own cues, so a planned character is reachable before they first speak.
  Shift+Tab still cycles back through element types, and a cue that already has
  text keeps the previous Tab behaviour.
- **Research recognises the script.** Research ▸ Characters and ▸ Locations now
  list what the draft contains, live: a cue or scene heading typed in the
  editor appears the next time you open the tab, with a count, an "Add N
  missing …" button and a per-name "Add NAME" button. Cards are never deleted;
  entities that exist only in Research are listed under "Only in Research".
  Clicking a folder row now selects its pane.

### Title page

- **The draft date and REVISED block are on the page.** Both were laid out on
  the KIT slot grid, whose A4 pitch put the draft date at −2.3 mm and the
  REVISED block at −16.6 mm — below the paper edge, so neither ever appeared
  in the preview or the PDF. The draft date is now anchored 22 mm above the
  page bottom and REVISED heading/name/date at 40/35/30 mm, keeping their KIT
  slot indexes and field order. Tests assert every non-empty title-page line
  lands inside the paper, with and without an active revision.

## Upgrade notes

- The page face defaults to IBM Plex Mono. Projects do not store a face, so
  opening an older file shows the new default until you pick Courier Prime in
  Settings.
- Existing saves need no migration: the new preferences are the app-wide
  `script_face=` and `paper_mono=` settings, and the title-page anchors change
  only where the date and REVISED block are drawn.
- Page count for single-space documents is unchanged; documents with runs of
  multiple spaces may now show those spaces and therefore differ from older
  exports, which is the intended fix.

## Deferred

Developer ID signing and notarisation, Windows code signing and auto-update
remain deferred (external credentials).
