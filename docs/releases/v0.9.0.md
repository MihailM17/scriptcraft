## Highlights

**ScriptCraft v0.9.0** is our biggest release yet. Alongside production revisions and page-exact screenwriting, ScriptCraft now ships a complete native **Shots** studio—taking a screenplay all the way from character-level coverage markup and interactive 2D top-down camera blocking to live timeline playback and print-ready **Director's Breakdown PDFs**, stored 100% locally inside your `.kitsp` project.

### 1. Shot Breakdown Workspace (`Color Map` & `Coverage Rails`)

- **Character-Level Coverage Markup:** Drag across any words, phrases, or multi-block passages (or double-click a word / triple-click a block) to assign shots without reflowing the screenplay. Overlapping coverage auto-merges into clean ranges, and **Move to** / **Unassign** let you re-assign any phrase in one click.
- **Hollywood Coverage Rails:** Switch between **Color Map** (inline tinted screenplay with margin shot badges) and **Coverage Rails** (classic lined-script vertical tracks per shot).
- **20-Color Studio Palette & Rich Shot Inspector:** Pick from 10 standard + 10 darker studio colors, configure shot size (`EWS`–`INS`), camera movement, lens presets (`24mm`–`85mm`), setup notes, reorder or delete shots in-scene, and see live estimated shot duration (`~Xs`).
- **Moment-Specific Camera Cues (`Moment Notes`):** Highlight any specific phrase *inside* an existing shot and click **`+ Note`** to attach a movement or action cue (e.g. *"Pan right to follow hand"*, *"Push in slowly"*) without splitting the shot. Noted phrases display a subtle amber underline + tooltip in the script, live HUD callouts during Timeline playback, and print in all PDF and CSV exports.
- **Anchored Script Zoom & Monochrome Paper:** Zoom the Shots script smoothly from `65%` to `350%` with zero text reflow and cursor/center-anchored scrolling, honoring your **Monochrome paper** setting.

### 2. Interactive 2D Top-Down Blocking Floor Plan & Split View

- **Auto-Populated Blocking Canvas:** Opening **Floor Plan** automatically places the scene's speaking cast and color-matched cameras (with physical focal-length FOV cones derived from each shot's lens and optional dolly/tracking paths).
- **True-to-Scale Cast, Architecture & Modeling Shapes:** Actors scale true-to-life against the dynamic metric scale bar (`2 m` / `1 m` / `50 cm` / `25 cm`). Place walls, doors, windows, tables, chairs, cars, and use **`+ Shapes`** to model custom props with **Square / Box**, **Circle / Ellipse**, **Triangle**, **Bed**, **Light**, and **Custom Prop**—all with editable labels.
- **Direct Manipulation & Precision Snapping:** Resize any prop directly via 4 edge handles, rotate via the rotation handle, drag a camera's aim dot to adjust both angle and FOV beam length, hold **`Ctrl`** to snap to grid/15° angles, toggle the **180° Line of Action**, and zoom deeply up to `450 px/m` anchored to the cursor.
- **Split View:** View and mark up the screenplay side by side with the live 2D Floor Plan.

### 3. Scene Beat Timeline & Active Camera Spotlight

- **Chronological Cut Scrubber:** Scrub or play through a scene's color-coded beat timeline (`<` prev cut, `Play / ||`, `>` next cut, draggable playhead, `M:SS / M:SS` timecode) directly beneath the Floor Plan.
- **Active Camera Spotlight:** Toggle **`All Cams` / `Timeline`** to illuminate **only the active camera** at the current moment in time (with live progress dots along dolly tracks and actor movement paths) while muting inactive cameras to gray. In **Split** mode, the active beat's exact text highlights live in sync, and any **Moment Note** on that beat pops up on the Floor Plan HUD.

### 4. Director's Breakdown PDF, Storyboards & Colored Script Exports

- **Full Director's Breakdown PDF (`Breakdown PDF…`):** Exports **Title Page → per-scene Visual Breakdown Sheets** (razor-sharp vector 2D overhead floor plans with grid, metric scale bar, shapes, actors, 180° axis, and cameras + shot cards with specs, `~Xs` duration, setup notes, moment cues, covered excerpts, and embedded storyboard thumbnails) **→ full Colored Script**.
- **Storyboard / Reference Frame Attachments:** Attach a PNG/JPEG sketch or reference photo per shot in the Shot Inspector—downscaled and persisted inside the `.kitsp` sidecar, cached on the GPU in-app, and embedded as image XObjects in the Director's Breakdown PDF.
- **Overhauled Colored Script & Shot List PDFs + CSV:** Full wrapped Latin & Cyrillic shot names in the wide `38 mm` left margin, amber moment-cue underlines and right-margin callouts, `EST` duration column, clean continuation headers, and opt-in **"Color script by shots"** inside the standard **Export Scenario** dialog.

### 5. Live Script-Edit Sync, Revision Alerts & Bold Character Cues

- **Live Span Synchronization:** Editing text in the **Script** tab automatically shifts character-level shot spans and moment notes so partial-line coverage stays aligned.
- **Revision-Change & Deleted-Text Alerts:** Shot cards and the Shot Inspector flag shots whose covered lines were modified in an active revision (e.g. `Blue rev`) or deleted.
- **Bold Speaking Character Cues:** New **Settings → Appearance → Bold character cues** option applies across the Script editor, Shots view, and exported PDFs.
